Survival guides and printable checklists

Plain, step-by-step guides for the emergencies people in the U.S. actually face, written from CDC, EPA, FEMA and U.S. Army guidance. Every guide has a checklist you can print, and the BugOut app carries the full library offline.

    The 72-hour checklist: water, food, shelter, first aid, tools and documents.

  • Power outage checklist

    How long to boil it, how much bleach per gallon, and which filters work.

  • 12 survival skills
