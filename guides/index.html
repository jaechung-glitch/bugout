Questions

What is the first thing to do to prepare for an emergency?

Make a plan with your household: how you'll get warnings, where you'll shelter, where you'd go if you had to leave, and how you'll reach each other. Then store water, 1 gallon per person per day for at least 3 days.

What are the most important survival skills?

Knowing what to do first. Get away from immediate danger, stop severe bleeding, get out of the weather, then deal with water, signaling and food, in that order.

Are these survival guides free?

Yes. Every guide and printable checklist on this page is free to read, print and share. The BugOut app, a free download for iPhone, carries a larger library that works with no signal.

Where does the information come from?

Each guide is written from public guidance by agencies such as FEMA, the CDC, the EPA and the National Weather Service, and from the U.S. Army Survival Manual, and lists its sources at the end.
