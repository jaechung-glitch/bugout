BUGOUT: Survival Guide & Gear

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BUGOUT require an internet connection?

No. BUGOUT is designed to work 100% offline. All survival guides, checklists, and tools, including Ask Wally, are stored and run directly on your device. You only need a connection to buy or restore BUGOUT Pro.

What does BUGOUT Pro include?

All 7 survival scenarios (Power Grid is Down and Nuclear Event are free), the compass, pressure and daylight cards on the Status tab (battery, GPS and connectivity are free), Ask Wally, the app's AI survival helper, and Survival Essentials, the master guide with the core method for 56 emergencies, from severe bleeding to house fires. The 70+ field guides and your own custom lists are free for everyone. Pro is an auto-renewing subscription you can manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. If you reinstall or get a new phone, open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Which iPhones does BUGOUT support?

BUGOUT runs on iOS 16 or later. Ask Wally writes answers on iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, on iOS 26 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings). On other iPhones, Wally shows the most relevant passages from the guides instead.

Why does BUGOUT need location access?

Location is used for the GPS display and to calculate sunrise/sunset times on the Status tab. Your location data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can deny location access and still use all other features.

How do I reset my checklist progress?

Open any scenario and tap the reset button at the top of the checklist to clear your progress for that scenario.

Can I create my own checklists?

Yes. Go to the My Lists tab and tap the "+" button to create a custom list. You can add items with quantities and track your progress.

The compass or barometer isn't working.

Not all devices have magnetometer or barometer sensors. If your device lacks these sensors, those cards on the Status tab will show as unavailable. For compass accuracy, try calibrating by moving your phone in a figure-8 pattern.

Will my data be lost if I delete the app?

Yes. All checklist progress, bookmarks, and custom lists are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.
