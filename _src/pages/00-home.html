Ask a survival question. Get an answer without a signal.

Wally is an AI survival assistant built into BugOut. Ask in plain words and it answers from BugOut's own library, leading with the core method from Survival Essentials and showing the passages each answer is based on.

  • Works anywhere: in airplane mode, in the backcountry, in a blackout. Wally runs entirely on your iPhone.
  • Private by design: your questions never leave your phone. No account, no tracking.
  • Grounded in the library: answers come from the CDC, FEMA and U.S. Army material in the app, not the open internet. Like any AI, Wally can make mistakes, so check the source before you rely on an answer.
  • Which iPhone models: written answers need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iOS 26). Other iPhone models get the most relevant passages from the guides. Wally is part of BugOut Pro.
