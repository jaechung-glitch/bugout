Privacy Policy

BugOut: Offline Survival Kit — Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

BugOut is designed with privacy as a core principle. The app works entirely offline. The only information that leaves your device is what's needed to sell and verify a BugOut Pro subscription, described under Purchases below, and none of it identifies you.

Data Collection

BugOut does not collect personal information that identifies you. Specifically:

  • No account creation or login is required
  • No advertising or tracking software is used, and we don't record how you use the app
  • Your location, sensor readings, lists, bookmarks and questions are never sent to any server
  • No cookies or advertising identifiers are used

Device Sensors

BugOut accesses the following device sensors to power its Status dashboard:

  • Location (GPS) — used to display your coordinates, altitude, speed, and to calculate sunrise/sunset times. Location data is processed entirely on-device and is never transmitted or stored beyond the current session.
  • Magnetometer & Accelerometer — used to display compass heading with tilt compensation. Data is processed on-device only.
  • Barometer — used to display atmospheric pressure and trends. Data is processed on-device only.
  • Battery — used to display battery level and charging estimates.
  • Network status — used to display connectivity status (online/offline).

All sensor data is processed locally on your device. None of this data is recorded, stored persistently, or transmitted anywhere.

Local Storage

BugOut stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Your checklist progress (which items you have checked off)
  • Your bookmarked library entries
  • Your custom lists and their items
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Whether you have BugOut Pro, so it keeps working offline

This data never leaves your device, except that your Pro status is confirmed with RevenueCat as described under Purchases. If you delete the app, all local data is permanently removed.

Ask Wally

Ask Wally, the AI helper in BugOut Pro, answers questions using the guides built into the app. Your questions are processed entirely on your device, by Apple's on-device model on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence. They are never sent to us, to Apple or to anyone else, and they are not saved: the conversation is cleared when you clear it or close the app.

Purchases

BugOut Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

BugOut uses RevenueCat, a subscription service, to confirm and restore BugOut Pro. When the app is online, it shares with RevenueCat:

  • Your purchase history for BugOut (what you bought and when, from Apple's receipt)
  • A random ID the app creates, which isn't connected to your name, Apple ID or any other identity
  • Your device's locale and currency, to show prices
  • Standard technical information any internet connection carries, such as your IP address

We use this only to provide your subscription and to see how many people subscribe. It isn't linked to your identity, isn't sold, and isn't used for advertising or tracking.

Age Checks

Where a law requires it, such as in Texas, BugOut asks Apple whether you're 18 or older before you can subscribe to BugOut Pro. Apple shares only an age range, such as "under 18" or "18 and over", never your birthday. BugOut uses it on your iPhone for that check and doesn't store it or send it anywhere.

If you're under 18 and installed BugOut before BugOut Pro was added, or BugOut's age rating has changed since you installed it, Apple asks a parent or guardian to approve the change before you can subscribe. BugOut keeps only, on your iPhone, whether the change was handled, when a parent was asked, and the app's last-seen age rating. Apple handles parental consent for downloads and purchases.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Apple's App Store and RevenueCat for purchases, BugOut does not use any third-party services, APIs, analytics platforms or advertising networks.

Children's Privacy

BugOut does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
